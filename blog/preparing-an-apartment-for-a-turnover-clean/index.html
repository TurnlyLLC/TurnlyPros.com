Preparing an apartment for a turnover clean

A clear handoff helps your cleaning team spend the visit cleaning instead of tracking down keys or guessing which rooms are ready. Before a resident moves in, walk through the apartment and make a short list for the team.

Confirm access and timing

  • Confirm the unit number, entry instructions, and where the team can park.
  • Share the move-in deadline and the time the apartment will be vacant.
  • Flag any work still scheduled for painters or maintenance so the cleaning visit happens in the right order.

Separate cleaning from maintenance

Note leaks, broken fixtures, or damaged flooring separately from cleaning requests. A photo and a room name are more useful than a general note that the apartment needs attention.

Agree on the scope

Call out priorities such as kitchen surfaces, bathrooms, and floors when you request the visit. Ask which appliances or interior surfaces are included so the team and property manager share the same expectations.
