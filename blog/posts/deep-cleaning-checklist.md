# The Deep Cleaning Checklist, Room by Room
![Bright, warm living room with a hand wiping the baseboard edge](https://cdn.marblism.com/QrzSZJ_2Sac.webp)

A deep cleaning checklist works best when you move room by room. It focuses on what everyday cleaning often skips: buildup on fixtures, edges, vents and baseboards; reachable appliance surfaces; light switches and door handles; and the places under or behind items you can safely move. Use the checklist below as a practical guide, then adjust it for your home, surfaces and priorities.

## What belongs on a deep cleaning checklist?

A deeper clean means giving extra attention to reachable areas that collect dust, grease, soap residue and everyday fingerprints. It does not mean moving heavy furniture, climbing unsafely or cleaning every hidden part of an appliance. Decide what is within reach and safe before you start.

Use the room-by-room list as a starting point. Add the tasks that matter in your home, such as cleaning a pet's favorite resting spot or wiping the inside of an emptied kitchen drawer. A printable deep cleaning checklist is most useful when it leaves room for notes about delicate surfaces, access and tasks you want to skip.

| Area | Room-by-room checklist |
|---|---|
| Kitchen | Wipe cabinet fronts and reachable tops; clean backsplash, counters and sink; wipe appliance exteriors and reachable interior surfaces; degrease the range hood exterior |
| Bathrooms | Clean shower, tub, sink and fixtures; address reachable grout and corners; wipe mirrors and cabinet fronts; clean behind the toilet and around its base |
| Bedrooms and living spaces | Dust reachable shelves, lamps and fan blades; wipe switches, handles and door frames; vacuum under cushions and reachable bed edges; clean reachable windowsills and baseboards |
| Floors and hallways | Dust floor edges and baseboards; vacuum rugs and reachable areas under furniture; clean door thresholds and handrails; vacuum, then damp-mop suitable hard floors |
| Extras | Dust vent covers and reachable blinds; wipe window tracks; clean inside accessible drawers or cabinets if emptied; wash removable throws or bedding as appropriate |

## What order should a room-by-room deep clean follow?

Start by clearing floors and counters enough to reach the surfaces you plan to clean. Gather cloths, a vacuum, suitable cleaners and a small bag for rubbish. Check product labels and surface-care instructions first, and test unfamiliar products in an inconspicuous spot.

Work from top to bottom so dust falls onto areas you have not cleaned yet. Dust or vacuum before using a damp cloth, then finish each room's floors last. Move from the back of the home toward the front so you are less likely to walk over clean floors.

Let cleaning products do their work while you move through the room. Apply them only as directed, wait for the label's stated time, then wipe or rinse as instructed. Never mix cleaning products, and ventilate the space when product directions call for it.

## How do you deep clean a kitchen?

Begin with reachable high spots, such as the top of the refrigerator or cabinet tops, if you can clean them safely. Dust vents and light fixtures, then wipe cabinet fronts, handles, backsplash and counters. Clean small appliances on the outside, and wipe reachable appliance surfaces inside only when they are empty and the manufacturer's directions allow it.

Give extra attention to the sink, faucet base and the edges around the cooktop, where residue can collect. Finish by vacuuming crumbs from floor edges and cleaning the floor. If you are cleaning inside an oven or refrigerator, treat that as a separate task and follow the appliance's instructions.

## What about bathrooms?

Apply suitable cleaner to the tub, shower and sink surfaces according to the label. While it sits, dust the vent cover and reachable shelves, wipe cabinet fronts and clean the mirror. Then scrub the fixtures, corners and reachable grout, and rinse or wipe as directed.

Finish with the toilet, including the base and the floor behind it if accessible. Use separate cloths for the toilet and other bathroom surfaces. Fold fresh towels only after the work is done, so they stay clean.

## How do you handle bedrooms and living spaces?

Dust from higher surfaces down: reachable fan blades, light fixtures, shelves and window ledges, followed by tables and other surfaces. Wipe door frames, switches and handles, then vacuum upholstery and under cushions. In bedrooms, change bedding and vacuum reachable floor space under the bed.

Move only light items you can shift safely, and return them after cleaning underneath. Avoid wet cleaning on surfaces that could be damaged; follow care labels for rugs, upholstery and bedding.

## What is easy to forget?

These small details are easy to miss during a quick pass through the home:

- Light switches, door handles and door frames
- Baseboards, room corners and floor edges
- Vent covers, windowsills and reachable window tracks
- Behind the toilet and around the bathroom faucet base
- Crumbs under sofa cushions and reachable furniture edges

A deep clean is also a good time to note spots that need special care, such as natural stone, unfinished wood or delicate finishes. Use a cleaner made for the surface, and avoid soaking wood floors or other materials that can be damaged by excess water.

## How long does a deep clean take?

It depends on the home's size, condition, number of bathrooms and the tasks you include. A home that gets regular upkeep may need less attention than one with weeks or months of buildup. Add time for optional tasks such as cleaning inside appliances or cabinets, washing blinds, or moving light furniture.

If you are splitting the work across days, choose a few rooms at a time and keep the same top-to-bottom order. A clear list helps you see what is done and what can wait.
