## What does a typical visit include?

A standard visit is meant to help keep the main rooms feeling cared for. The checklist should be shaped around the home and priorities you agree on before work begins. It often focuses on:

- Kitchen counters, sinks, and agreed appliance surfaces
- Bathroom sinks, tubs or showers, toilets, mirrors, and fixtures
- Reachable surfaces and everyday touchpoints
- Bedrooms and living spaces, including agreed surfaces
- Vacuuming carpets and cleaning hard floors by surface type

The exact scope matters. Inside appliances, cabinet interiors, wall washing, and other detailed tasks may not be included in a standard clean. Ask about specific requests in advance so they can be confirmed and reflected in the plan.

## What should I do before the cleaner arrives?

You do not need to clean your home before a cleaning visit. A little planning can make it easier to focus the visit on the work you want done.

Share practical details ahead of time, including how to enter, where to park, and whether there are pets at home. If a surface or item needs special care, point it out. You can also mention rooms you would like prioritized and any areas you would prefer the cleaner to leave untouched.

Clearing floors and the surfaces you want cleaned can help make those areas accessible. If you have delicate items or valuables, put them somewhere safe before the visit. The goal is not to prepare a perfect home; it is to help the cleaner follow the agreed scope.

## Everyday upkeep or deeper reset?

The right service depends on whether you need help maintaining a home or giving it more detailed attention. Here is a simple way to think about the options:

| Service | A good fit when | What to discuss |
|---|---|---|
| Everyday upkeep | You want regular care for kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, living spaces, and floors | Priority rooms, visit frequency, and the checklist for each visit |
| Deeper reset | Your home needs more attention than routine maintenance provides | Buildup, detailed tasks, and any areas needing extra focus |
| Move-in or move-out | You are preparing a home for a move or a new occupant | Whether appliance interiors, cabinets, closets, or other specific tasks are included |

A first visit may need a different scope from later upkeep if buildup has collected or the home has not had a detailed clean in a while. That extra attention is why it helps to describe the home honestly and confirm the checklist before work begins. A deeper service is not automatically the right fit for every first visit; the plan should reflect the home’s condition and your priorities.
